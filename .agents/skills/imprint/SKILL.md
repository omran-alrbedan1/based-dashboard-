# Red Power Imprint

- Arabic-first RTL workshop-management product.
- Use near-black/charcoal surfaces, zinc/white text, metallic-gray borders, and Red Power red `#e10613` for primary actions, selected navigation, and urgent emphasis.
- Keep the tone precise, premium, performance-focused, and operationally clear.
- Use real workshop terminology; avoid delivery, vendor, product, and payment vocabulary.
- Brand through theme tokens and feature composition, not by hard-coding Red Power decisions into reusable primitives.

The receipt reference defines the first-card sections: customer information, vehicle information, visit reason, customer complaint, vehicle condition on receipt, items in car, required work, customer approval, garage information, and expected delivery. Keep every design responsive and RTL-legible.
